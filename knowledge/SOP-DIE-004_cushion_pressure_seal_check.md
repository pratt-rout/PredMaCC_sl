# SOP-DIE-004: Hydraulic Cushion Pressure and Seal Check

> SAMPLE / PLACEHOLDER CONTENT for demonstration. Replace with your plant's approved procedure.

## Purpose
Maintain blank hold-down force and detect cushion seal degradation early (failure mode: Cushion Pressure Loss).

## Limits
- Normal cushion pressure: about 12 bar.
- Warning: below 9.5 bar or above 15 bar. Critical: below 8.5 bar or above 17 bar.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the gauge and pressure sensor agree within 0.3 bar.
2. Check for oil weeping at cushion cylinder seals and fittings.
3. Measure pressure decay over 60 seconds with the press idle. A drop above 1.0 bar indicates a leaking seal.
4. Replace seals if leakage is confirmed, then bleed the circuit and recalibrate to 12 bar.
5. Rebalance blank-holder pressure across cushion pins to within 0.5 bar.

## Follow-up
A falling pressure trend together with a drop in die force usually points to seal wear. Schedule seal replacement at the next planned stop.
