# SOP-DIE-002: Die Surface Galling and Crack Inspection

> SAMPLE / PLACEHOLDER CONTENT for demonstration. Replace with your plant's approved procedure.

## Purpose
Detect surface galling (adhesive wear) and fatigue cracks in die inserts before they cause defective panels or a die failure (failure modes: Surface Galling, Die Crack).

## Early indicators from sensors
- Surface galling: die force rising 5-10 percent above baseline, vibration up, die temperature up 10 C or more.
- Die crack: die strain rising above 1,500 microstrain together with vibration above 2.0 g.

## Procedure
1. Stop the press, apply lockout/tagout, and clean the die face.
2. Inspect draw radii and binder surfaces for scoring, transfer of sheet material, or discolouration.
3. Apply dye penetrant to insert corners and radii. Any linear indication is a suspected crack.
4. For galling: polish affected areas with stones (400 then 800 grit) and reapply die lubricant.
5. For a confirmed crack: tag the die out of service and raise a critical work order for insert replacement.
6. After repair, run a tryout of 50 panels and inspect for surface defects.

## Notes
Do not return a die with a confirmed crack to production, even if sensor readings look normal.
