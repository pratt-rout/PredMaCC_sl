# SOP-DIE-003: Die Thermal Management

> SAMPLE / PLACEHOLDER CONTENT for demonstration. Replace with your plant's approved procedure.

## Purpose
Prevent thermal distortion that shifts die dimensions and degrades panel quality (failure mode: Thermal Distortion).

## Limits
- Normal die surface temperature: about 45 C during production.
- Warning: above 60 C. Critical: above 70 C.

## Procedure
1. If temperature exceeds 60 C, reduce press speed by 20 percent and check the cooling circuit flow.
2. Inspect cooling channels for blockage or scale. Flush with approved descaler if flow is low.
3. If temperature exceeds 70 C, stop production and let the die cool to below 50 C before restart.
4. After cooling, check proximity (die clearance) because thermal growth reduces clearance.
5. Verify that lubricant application is even; dry spots raise friction heat.

## Follow-up
Log the event and open a work order to inspect cooling channels if the alert repeats within one shift.
