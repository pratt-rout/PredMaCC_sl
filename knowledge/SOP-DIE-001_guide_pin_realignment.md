# SOP-DIE-001: Guide Pin and Bushing Realignment

> SAMPLE / PLACEHOLDER CONTENT for demonstration. Replace with your plant's approved procedure.

## Purpose
Restore punch-to-die clearance when proximity readings drift or ram position deviation increases, typically caused by guide pin wear (failure mode: Misalignment).

## Trigger conditions
- Proximity (die clearance) outside 1.2 - 3.8 mm, or trending toward either limit.
- Ram position deviation above 0.8 mm (warning) or 1.0 mm (critical).
- Vibration rising together with proximity drift.

## Procedure
1. Stop the press and apply lockout/tagout. Confirm the ram is at top dead centre.
2. Measure guide pin and bushing clearance at all four corners with a feeler gauge. Record values.
3. Replace any pin or bushing with clearance above 0.05 mm over nominal.
4. Re-shim the die halves to restore parallelism within 0.02 mm.
5. Run 20 trial strokes at reduced speed and check proximity and ram position readings.
6. Restore full speed only when proximity is 2.0 - 3.0 mm and ram deviation is below 0.3 mm.

## Follow-up
Create a corrective work order noting the measured clearances. Re-inspect after 5,000 strokes.
